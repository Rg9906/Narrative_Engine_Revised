Chapter I: The Evening Tea.
In the grand drawing room of Whitmore Manor, Sebastian was sipping tea. Talia was upstairs in her private bedchamber resting. Laurie entered the drawing room, holding a sealed bottle of fresh milk. He set the sealed milk bottle on the side table in the drawing room. Sebastian, feeling restless, stood up, grabbed his overcoat, and walked out of the drawing room toward the carriage house. Meanwhile, Laurie headed into the empty study to read.
