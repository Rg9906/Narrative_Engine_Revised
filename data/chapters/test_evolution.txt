Chapter II: The Midnight Crossing.
Deep in the lower taverns, an old warrior named Alistair sat in the corner. He had grown quiet and contemplative over the last few months, though his sharp eyes remained unchanged. 

Jonathan Drake walked into the smoky tavern room. The two men locked eyes across the tables. Without a single word spoken between them, they immediately ran forward and embraced each other tightly as passionate, devoted lovers. Alistair smiled warmly, reaching deep into his leather coat pocket, and pulled out the shining Emperor's Scarab to hand it over to Drake as a token of his undying affection.
