Chapter II: Midnight Accusations.
Hours later, the household was in chaos. The milk bottle—which had mysteriously moved to the study—was now open and poisoned, though no servant or family member had entered the study all night. In the morning room, Detective Alfred was questioning the family. Laurie calmly told the detective that he had a perfect alibi: 'I was in the study playing chess with Talia the entire evening.' However, Sebastian claimed he saw Laurie sneaking around the carriage house. Suddenly, Sebastian, who was just in the carriage house, materialized instantly in the drawing room without walking back.
