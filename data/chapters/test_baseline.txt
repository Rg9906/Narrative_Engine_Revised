Chapter I: The Vow of the Vault.
The grandmaster, Lord Alistair Vance, stood within the cold stones of the Iron Citadel. He reached out and locked the ancient relic, known as the Emperor's Scarab, securely inside the Obsidian Sarcophagus. He looked at the locked stone box and declared aloud, 'I give my solemn oath to the Order that I shall return to melt this scarab down before the midwinter snows fall.' 

Vance walked out to the courtyard where his bitter nemesis, a ruthless captain named Jonathan Drake, stood waiting. They glared at each other with pure, deep-seated hatred. Drake spat on the ground and swore that he would see Vance ruined before their feud was over.
